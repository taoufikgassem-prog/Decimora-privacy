Decimora – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

App: Decimora (Android, package app.decimora.matrix), version 1.0.0

Developer / publisher: Taoufik Gassem

Privacy contact: taoufik.gassem@gmail.com

Summary

Decimora is an offline decision-making tool. It does not collect, transmit or

share any personal or user data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data the app handles (on your device only)

  • Decisions you create: title, optional description, creation and modification dates.
  • Options, criteria, importance levels, notes and scores you enter.
  • Preferences: theme and language.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is used

only to show and calculate your decisions inside the app. It is never sent to the

developer or to anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request the Android internet permission and cannot connect to any server.
  • It has no account, sign-in, cloud sync, backend or remote database.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and does

not use the Advertising ID.

  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files,

calendar, SMS, phone, Bluetooth or notifications.

  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • It contains no AI and no in-app purchases.

Security and retention

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps.

It is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and

device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app, so your decisions are not

copied to other services or devices (and are therefore not restored automatically

on a new device).

Your control and deletion

  • Delete an individual decision from the Decisions list.
  • Delete everything with Settings › Delete all data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

Because the developer never receives your data, the developer holds no copy to

access, correct or delete. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

Children

Decimora is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It

collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated

before that version is released and the effective date above will change.
